Delicious Chocolate Chip Cookies

These homemade chocolate chip cookies are chewy, gooey, and packed with rich chocolate flavor. Perfect for any sweet tooth!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 3/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. Cream together the softened butter, granulated sugar, and brown sugar until smooth.
  3. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then stir in the vanilla extract.
  4. Gradually blend in the all-purpose flour and chocolate chips until a dough forms.
  5. Scoop rounded tablespoons of dough onto the baking sheet and bake for 10 to 12 minutes.
